                        34. Patient.identifier
                        Definition

                        The patient’s identification number. In Dutch transfer situations, use of the social security number (BSN) must comply with the Use of Social Security Numbers in Healthcare Act (Wbsn-z). In other situations, other number systems can be used, such as internal hospital patient numbers, foreign identification numbers and passport numbers.

                        ShortPatientIdentificationNumber
                        NoteThis is a business identifier, not a resource identifier (see discussion)
